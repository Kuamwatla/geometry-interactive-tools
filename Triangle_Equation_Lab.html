EQUATION LAB

1 · Define the vertices

Edit coordinates or drag A, B, C. Drag inside the triangle to move the whole figure.

2 · Translate the triangle

(x, y) → (x + h, y + k)

h: horizontal change · k: vertical change

Drag a vertex · Drag the triangle · Arrow keys move a focused vertex

3 · Equations of the sides

A triangle has three sides. Each side lies on a line with its own equation.

Read the equation as a relationship between X and Y.

Every point on that line satisfies the equation. The two endpoints limit the line to one side of the triangle.

Displayed values are rounded to two decimals.
